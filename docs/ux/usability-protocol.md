# First-time usability protocol (ACC-09)

ACC-09: ten first-time users, ≥ 8 must describe the product correctly after a
guided first session. This is the study instrument; running it needs the
client build (ACC-08 order: store-acceptable build first, then this study).

## Participants

10 adults (BR-03: adults-only product) in the launch metro, recruited outside
the team, mixed device tiers within the NFR-28 floors (iOS 16 / Android 10+).
30 minutes per session, moderated, screen + audio recorded with consent.

## Script

1. **Cold open (5 min).** Hand over the phone with the app freshly installed.
   No explanation. Ask them to sign in and "do what the app seems to be for."
2. **Core tasks (20 min), unassisted unless stuck > 2 min:**
   - create a profile (3–20 interests — watch whether the floor explanation
     lands, FR-08/FR-11),
   - publish presence / check in at the venue (FR-48),
   - interpret a match surface (FR-76) and the constellation picker (FR-81),
   - express interest → open a conversation (consent ladder),
   - find the safety tray unprompted at least once (BR-07, ≤ 2 taps),
   - read a structured transfer card if the scenario includes one (AMD-PAY-03:
     amount/currency/fee/state only).
3. **Comprehension question (5 min).** "In your own words — what is this
   product, and who can see you?" Scored correct only if the answer captures
   BOTH: proximity-based discovery AND consent-gated visibility (nobody sees
   you before you publish, nobody identifiable before mutual open).

## Scoring

- Pass bar: **≥ 8 of 10** correct comprehension answers (ACC-09).
- Secondary: task completion per task, time-to-first-publish, and every
  instance of a dead end (TR-32 violation — any dead end found here is a
  launch blocker regardless of the score).
- Failures classify: comprehension failure → onboarding/copy problem
  (discoverySkeleton, FR-80); task failure → surface contract problem.
  Record verbatim quotes; no numeric-only notes.

## Evidence record

One record per session (score sheet + recording reference + notes) filed with
the pilot acceptance pack (ACC-01). The study re-runs after any onboarding
redesign; a passing score expires after 12 months.
